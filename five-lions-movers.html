Safety

Safety isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

Every Five Lions ride is built on a safety code our riders carry as a card and live by every shift.

Two helmets, every ride

One for the rider, one for you — sanitized between trips, replaced every twelve months.

Live GPS oversight

Every bike streams its position to our Kigali dispatch hub in real time.

Verified riders

Identity, RURA permit, motorcycle papers and clean record check, renewed every six months.

One-tap SOS

A one-tap emergency button alerts our dispatch and your trusted contacts simultaneously.

Trained rider corps

Mandatory defensive-riding course, annual recertification, and quarterly bike inspections.

24/7 human dispatch

Real people on the line in Kinyarwanda, English, and French — for every ride, every shift.
